# Second Opinion Localization Terminology

This project ships Turkish, English and Spanish at implementation time. Turkish is the source language, but translations should be native product copy rather than literal word-for-word output.

| Medical imaging | Tıbbi görüntüleme | Medical imaging | Imagen médica |

## Clinical And Legal Tone

Avoid diagnosis guarantees, emergency-care substitution language, success rates, fake certification claims, fake institutional affiliations, and overly confident clinical promises. Use careful phrases such as "may be useful", "expert perspective", "structured report", and "not emergency care".

Legal and medical copy in `kvkk`, `privacy`, `terms`, `cookies`, blog medical notices, report delivery disclaimers and security claims must be reviewed by qualified legal and clinical stakeholders before production.
